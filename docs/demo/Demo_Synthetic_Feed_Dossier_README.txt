DEMO SYNTHETIC DOSSIER – FEED ADDITIVE (6-PHYTASE)
Title: Demo Synthetic Feed Dossier
Source reference: FEED-2026-38110 (non-confidential version published on Open EFSA)
Prepared: 28 November 2026

CONTENTS AND PURPOSE
56 files (556 PDF pages, one .xlsx, one .docx) from the published, sanitised application for a 6-phytase produced by
Trichoderma reesei CBS 153575 (zootechnical additive, digestibility enhancer, all poultry and all pigs; formulations 30 L and 65 G),
repackaged under short, Windows-safe folder and file names. It is a test fixture for KEvidence, not an application, and no EFSA
endorsement or regulatory approval is claimed.

WHAT IS SYNTHETIC
1. Identity. The applicant, its group companies, the trade name, production sites, laboratories, addresses, telephone numbers,
   e-mail addresses and logos were replaced throughout, in text and in scanned pages:
     applicant ............. Acme Feed Enzymes B.V., Willem Proefstraat 4, 2333 ZZ Leiden, The Netherlands
     product ............... AcmePHY® PLUS 30 L / AcmePHY® PLUS 65 G (30 T: separate application, not included)
     group companies ....... Acme Animal Nutrition, Acme Health & Biosciences, Acme Nutrition & Biosciences, Acme Enzymes Oy
     sites ................. Plant L (Esimerkkilä, FI), Plant S (Satamala, FI), Acme Feed Technology Centre (Aarhus, DK)
   All persons are fictional (e.g. Dr Ana Ferreira-Holm, Regulatory Affairs Manager). The EURL official is shown by role only.
   Telephone numbers are non-operational; e-mail addresses use the reserved .example domain.
2. Redactions. Detection found 750 opaque areas (text pages and scans). 703 were EFSA redactions
   and were replaced by synthetic content; the other 47 were dark gel photographs (rDNA report) and GHS
   pictograms, left as they are. The synthetic content is consistent with the data left visible:
     - formulation ranges and TOS of the final products (Section II 2.1.3), derived from the TOS/activity ratio of the three
       concentrate lots whose CoAs are visible (ECP1901, ECP2101, ECP2102);
     - manufacturing description, flow charts, raw materials, in-process controls and specifications (Section II 2.3);
     - feed and premix compositions, raw stability/homogeneity/processing data, background phytase levels, each reproducing the
       recoveries, CVs and means stated in the visible summaries of the study reports;
     - validation appendices (LOD/LOQ blanks, repeatability/reproducibility, trueness, linearity) matching the visible CVs and limits;
     - strain characterisation details: in-house strain designation TR-PHY-0451, WGS metrics, antiSMASH tables, genetic-modification
       structure (Annex_II_16: 95 pages of synthetic annotation tables), rDNA and viable-strain test details, a synthetic mature
       protein sequence (not the real phytase sequence);
     - reagent identities, signatures, names, dates of signature and lot numbers.
   Filled values are realistic but invented. They are not the applicant's data and must not be read as such.
3. Every page carries the banner "DEMO SYNTHETIC DOSSIER". PDF metadata and XMP were replaced; Office metadata (authors, custom
   properties, an absolute SharePoint path) was removed. Scanned pages received an OCR text layer, so the dossier is searchable.

WHAT IS NOT SYNTHETIC
Scientific text and numbers that EFSA left visible are unchanged (batch activities, summaries, conclusions, references). Public
identifiers remain: strain accession CBS 153575, the earlier strain CBS 146250, Regulation (EU) 2023/1167, EFSA Journal
2022;20(11):7610, EFSA pre-application ID and the EURL reference. These identify the public source dossier; changing the applicant
name does not make it untraceable. Citations naming the former holder were rewritten with the fictional name and therefore no
longer match the published titles.

LIMITS FOR TESTING
The source is a new-strain application that relies on an earlier FEEDAP assessment: toxicology, tolerance and efficacy studies are
referenced, not included. The production strain is a genetically modified microorganism: KEvidence's FEEDAP characterisation MVP
covers chemical characterisation; production-strain characterisation is out of its scope and is expected to be flagged.
